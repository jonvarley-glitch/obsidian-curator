title: Weekly Review Assistant

Help me complete my weekly review by analysing my vault activity over the past 7 days. Provide:

1. **Meetings Summary**: List all meetings this week with key takeaways from each
2. **Completed Tasks**: What got done this week
3. **Outstanding Items**: Tasks or follow-ups still open
4. **Customer Activity**: Which customers were active this week and any notable developments
5. **Inbox Status**: Any notes in 00-Inbox that still need filing
6. **Suggested Priorities**: Based on open items and upcoming deadlines, recommend top 3 priorities for next week

Be concise. Use bullet points. Flag anything that looks overdue or at risk.
