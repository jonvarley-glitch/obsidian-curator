type: prompt
title: Meeting Follow-up Email
---

Draft a professional follow-up email based on the meeting note I'm currently viewing. The email should:

1. **Thank attendees** for their time
2. **Summarise key discussion points** in clear bullet points
3. **List action items** with owners and deadlines where specified
4. **Outline next steps** and any upcoming meetings or milestones
5. **Attach or reference** any materials discussed

Keep the tone professional but warm. Use plain language -- avoid jargon unless it was used in the meeting. Format for easy scanning with headers and bullet points.
