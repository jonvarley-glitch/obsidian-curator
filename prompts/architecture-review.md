title: Architecture Review
---

Review the architecture proposed in the current note against patterns and history captured elsewhere in this vault.

Provide:

1. **Summary** — one paragraph capturing what's being proposed.
2. **Strengths** — bullets, each citing why with a `[[link]]` to vault evidence where possible.
3. **Concerns / Risks** — likelihood and impact for each. Reference any `system` notes, project history, or competition notes that ground the concern.
4. **Open Questions** — clarifying questions whose answers would change the assessment.
5. **Pattern Match** — note where the proposed approach matches or diverges from patterns recorded in `04-Knowledge/` or prior projects in `02-Projects/`.
6. **Recommendation** — go / iterate / rethink, with a one-sentence justification.

Rules:

- Ground every claim in vault content where possible. If you assert something without a citation, flag it as your inference.
- Be direct about weaknesses; do not soften clear concerns.
- Treat `[[wikilinks]]` as authoritative anchors — link to the most relevant note for each point.
